Shop tools

Foilbay includes the tools card shops usually pay apps for: a deck list importer, a buylist page, set checklists, quick view, a sticky add-to-cart bar, star ratings, recently viewed products and menu panels with collection images.

Deck list importer

Shoppers paste a deck list and add every card you stock to the cart in one click.

  1. Online Store › Pages › Add page. Title it Deck importer. Under Theme template, choose page.deck-importer.
  2. Add the page to your menu.

Accepted formats, one card per line: 4 Card name, 4x Card name, Card name x4. Section headers like Deck: or Sideboard:, set codes like (EMF) 4 or EMF 4, and foil markers like *F* are skipped.

How cards are matched. Each name is looked up with your store's own search. A product matches when its title starts with the card name, followed by anything in brackets or after a dash, bar or dot. So all of these match Emberlynx:

  • Emberlynx
  • Emberlynx - Emberfall 004/165
  • Emberlynx (Holo) and Emberlynx [Promo]
  • Emberlynx · PSA 10

Accents and apostrophes are ignored. When several printings match, shoppers can pick from all of them.

Stock. The importer reads each version's stock. If someone asks for 4 copies and you have 2 Near Mint and 3 Lightly Played, it fills the order with 2 NM and 2 LP, using the Default condition choice (cheapest first, or best condition first). If there still aren't enough, it shows a row saying how many are missing. Shoppers can change any version or quantity, and quantities can't go above what you have. Products that continue selling when out of stock have no limit.

Cards you don't stock are listed with a link to search your store.

Buylist (sell to us)

  1. Create a page called Sell to us with the template page.buylist.
  2. In the theme editor, set your Cash payout and Store credit payout percentages and the condition choices.

Shoppers add one row per card: name, set and number, condition and quantity. Submissions arrive in your store's contact email, tagged buylist, with every card on its own line. The page works without JavaScript too: shoppers can type their list into a text box.

Set checklist

A set checklist shows every card in a set in card number order, with dashed slots for numbers you don't stock. Collectors use it to fill gaps in their binder.

  1. Make a collection for the set, for example Emberfall with the condition Tag is equal to set:Emberfall.
  2. Link to the checklist by adding ?view=checklist to the collection URL, for example /collections/emberfall?view=checklist. Shopify then shows the collection with the collection.checklist template. You can put the link in a menu or in the collection description.
  3. Card numbers come from the tcg.number metafield or a number: tag. Write them as 004/165 so the checklist knows the set has 165 cards, or enter Cards in set in the section settings.

Shoppers can show all cards, only in-stock cards, only missing numbers or only foils.

Quick view

Products with options, like sleeves in several colors, show a Quick view button in product grids. It opens the product in a pop-up with the variant picker and add to cart. Turn it off in Theme settings › Product cards › Show quick view. Singles keep their condition chips instead.

Sticky add-to-cart bar

Add the Sticky add to cart bar block to the product page. The bar slides in once shoppers scroll past the buy buttons or condition table.

Star ratings

Foilbay shows stars on product cards and the product page from Shopify's standard product rating fields (reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count). Install any reviews app that fills those fields, such as Shopify Product Reviews, Judge.me or Loox. Stars are hidden for products without ratings. Turn them off in Theme settings › Product cards › Show star rating, or remove the Rating block on the product page.

Recently viewed

Add the Recently viewed section to the product page or any template. It lists the products a shopper looked at most recently, stored in their browser. Nothing is shown until they have viewed at least one other product.

Menu panels with images

In Header › Menu panels with images, dropdowns open as a full-width panel that shows each collection's image. Set collection images in Products › Collections. Links to other pages show a holographic placeholder.
