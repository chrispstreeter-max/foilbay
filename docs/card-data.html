Card data

Foilbay shows a card's set, card number, rarity, finish and condition on product tiles, in the singles list view and on the product page. It reads each value from the first place it finds it, so you can use whatever your inventory tool already exports.

Shopify's trading card category

Set each card's Category to Arts & Entertainment › Hobbies & Creative Arts › Collectibles › Collectible Trading Cards › Gaming Cards. Shopify then adds fields for Rarity, Condition, Grading, Card attributes and Trading card packaging. Foilbay shows them automatically, and Search & Discovery can filter by them.

Conditions as variants (recommended)

Give each single a variant option called Condition with values such as Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, Heavily Played and Damaged. Add a second option, for example Finish (Normal / Reverse holo), if you stock both.

  • Each in-stock condition becomes its own price chip with quick add on product tiles.
  • The product page shows a condition table: one row per variant with stock, price, quantity and add to cart.
  • With Theme settings › Card data › Abbreviate conditions on, Near Mint shows as NM, Lightly Played as LP, and so on. Values like "Near mint (NM)" use the abbreviation in brackets.

If your option has a different name, add it to Theme settings › Card data › Condition option names.

Importing by CSV

Tags are the quickest way to add set, number and rarity in bulk. In Shopify's product CSV, put them in the Tags column:

set:Emberfall, number:004/165, rarity:Holo Rare, foil

Pre-orders

Tag a product preorder to show a Pre-order badge and change its button to Pre-order. Turn on Continue selling when out of stock for the variant if you take orders before stock arrives.
